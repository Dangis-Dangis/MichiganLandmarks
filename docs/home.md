# Help

Michigan Landmarks is an unofficial, offline-first guide to Michigan lighthouses, historical markers, National Register sites, state and national parks, and museums.

Use the topics below, or search from the Help home screen in the app. Legal, privacy, and data-source attribution live under [Legal & Privacy](LEGAL.md#privacy).
